# Blagaj public webcam stills — 9 September 2026

Archive stills recovered from a public tourist webcam at Blagaj, Bosnia and
Herzegovina, covering the morning of 9 September 2026.

## What the camera is

A pan/tilt/zoom (PTZ) tourist webcam overlooking the Buna spring (Vrelo Bune)
and the Blagaj Tekija. On-screen branding: "Blagaj — moj radio" (upper left)
and "moja tv" (upper right, BH Telecom's MojaTV IPTV service), with a MojaTV
weather and promotional ticker along the bottom.

The camera moves between preset views. Observed presets:

- tight framing on the tekke and the cave mouth at the spring
- a wide view up the Buna canyon toward the mountains and village
- a wider view of the tekke, spring, bridges and village

## Why these were collected

The subject event — an engagement at Stjepan Grad (Blagaj Castle), the hilltop
fortress above the village — took place at approximately 07:00 on 9 September
2026. No personal photographs were taken.

## Important limitations

- **This camera does not cover the fortress.** Every observed preset looks at
  the tekke, the spring or the canyon. The fortress courtyard, where the event
  took place, does not appear in any recovered frame. This webcam therefore
  almost certainly did not capture the event itself.
- **The archive interval is roughly 50 minutes**, and 07:00 falls in the gap
  between the 06:34 and 07:25 captures. The sampling interval is a property of
  the archive these were pulled from, not of the camera, so the operator may
  hold continuous video for that window.
- The camera's mounting point has not been established. Reading the frame
  geometry — the cliff face rises above the tekke in shot — it appears to sit
  across the valley rather than on the fortress hill, but this is inference
  from the imagery and is not confirmed.
- The exact archive source URL and the identity of the archiving service were
  not recorded at collection time and remain to be confirmed.

These frames are retained because 06:34 and 07:25 bracket the event and are
contemporaneous images of the location that morning.
